# Weekend Bread

A simple loaf that needs no special equipment.

## Ingredients

- 500 g bread flour
- 350 ml lukewarm water
- 10 g salt
- 5 g instant yeast

## Steps

1. Mix the flour, salt and yeast in a large bowl.
2. Add the water and stir until no dry flour remains.
3. Cover and let it rest for **12 to 18 hours** at room temperature.
4. Shape the dough into a ball and place it in a floured towel.
5. Bake in a preheated pot at 240 °C for 30 minutes with the lid on, then 15 minutes without.

> Tip: the longer the rest, the more flavor the bread develops.

| Step | Time |
|------|------|
| Mix | 5 min |
| Rest | 12–18 h |
| Bake | 45 min |
